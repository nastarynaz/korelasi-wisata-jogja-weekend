Are tourism areas in Yogyakarta more active on weekends? A seven-case study combines JogjaPulse regional device presence with documented tourism references. Six areas meet coverage requirements: Kokap and Kretek have positive weekend differences in both sample periods; four other cases change direction. These are regional observations, not attraction visitor counts.
